# Asset identity images

Company PNGs: https://github.com/nvstly/icons/tree/main/ticker_icons
Cryptocurrency PNGs: https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons/tree/master/128/color
Cryptocurrency Icons project: CC0-1.0; see https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Marks belong to their respective owners. Display is for asset identification only,
not endorsement, partnership, or a claim of trading availability. Confirm applicable
trademark usage requirements before public commercial launch.

Regional Toyota and Tencent examples use TM and TCEHY. Fund identities use
the bundled ETF images listed below, including Vanguard funds. No logos are fabricated.

Cryptocurrency images are served locally from ../crypto-logos (CC0 license included).
Coins without a bundled logo show an explicit ticker; no runtime hotlink is used.

Display adaptation: white-only transparent marks (AAPL, ASML, IVZ, BLK,
TCEHY) are rendered in charcoal with original alpha preserved so they remain
visible on the white identity badges in both light and dark themes.

ETF logos (VTI, VOO, BND, QQQ, SCHD, TLT):
https://github.com/tdrmk/stock-icons/tree/main/ticker_icons
These replace the previous issuer aliases and Vanguard ticker-only fallbacks.
